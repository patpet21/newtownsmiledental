FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Before your visit.

Are new patients welcome?

Yes. Use the appointment request form or call 718-393-0100 to ask for the next available new-patient visit.

Does the office accept my dental insurance?

The practice works with several dental benefit plans. Because participation and coverage can vary by plan, contact the office with your carrier name so the team can verify your specific benefits.

Is an online request a confirmed appointment?

No. Online requests are sent to the office for review. Your appointment is confirmed only after a team member contacts you with the date and time.

What should I send through the website?

Only basic contact and scheduling information. Do not submit Social Security numbers, insurance member IDs, diagnoses, medical history or other sensitive health information through this general website form.
